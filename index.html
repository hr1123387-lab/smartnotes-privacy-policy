Local Notes Privacy Policy

Last Updated: June 9, 2026

Overview

Local Notes is a simple note-taking application. Your privacy is important to us, and this app is designed to keep your data on your device.

Data Storage

All notes you create are stored locally on your device using on-device storage. Your notes remain on your phone or tablet and are not uploaded to any server, cloud service, or third party.

Data Collection

This app does not collect, transmit, store, or share personal data. We do not use analytics, advertising, tracking, or account registration.

Permissions

Local Notes does not request unnecessary permissions. The app works entirely offline and does not require access to your contacts, location, camera, microphone, or other sensitive device features.

Data Deletion

You can delete individual notes at any time from within the app. Uninstalling the app removes all locally stored notes from your device.

Children's Privacy

This app does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under the age of 13.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

If this privacy policy is updated, the revised version will be published on this page and may also be included in future app updates.

Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact the developer using the email address provided on the Google Play Store listing.
